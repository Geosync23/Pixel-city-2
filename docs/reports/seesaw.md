# The seesaw and the swings: sitting on them properly (owner msg 218)

Worker notes, 2026-11-17. Not committed; for the lead to review.

## Cause

Two separate faults, both in how the playground is offered to the player (`src/sim/activities.js`, `buildCity`):

1. **"Have a Swing" at the Playground sat the player in the air.** The playground poi (65, 185) carries the tag `swing`. A poi with that tag and no seat of its own got the default seat "the poi's own position, 0.45 up, facing dir 1". So the player sat in the chair pose in the middle of the playground, on nothing, next to the seesaw's raised end. That is the picture the owner saw.
2. **The seesaw and the swing set were benches.** Both props have seats, so the generic "bench props" loop gave them a "Sit" spot with the bench menu (People-Watch, Sketch the Scene, ...). Nothing rode them, and nothing moved.

The seesaw's seat data (`prop.park.play.seesaw`: heights, facing the middle) was already right. What was missing was a way to ride it, the astride pose, and the movement.

## Change

- **Playground poi:** "Have a Swing" now seats the player on a real seat of the swing set standing there (nearest free seat of the nearest prop tagged `swings` within 6 tiles), facing out.
- **Seesaw:** tapping it offers a new activity, **Ride the See-saw** (`seesaw`, tag `seesaw`, 5 to 20 minutes, fun 26 an hour, no mini-game). The rider sits astride at an end seat, facing the middle, hands on the handle.
- **Swing set:** tapping it offers "Have a Swing" at its seats (with the existing "Swing higher" mini-game).
- **Movement** (`src/sim/playground.js`, new, same approach as `sim/rocking.js`): while somebody sits on an end the seesaw plank is stepped through five tilts along a 2.6 s sine and each rider is kept at the height of their end; when the last rider gets up it swings on briefly and settles to its old resting picture. A swing seat with a rider swings out and back in five steps and the rider moves with it. City scene only; a piece further than 46 tiles from the camera costs one distance test a frame.
- **Astride pose** (`pose.straddle` in `src/art/chars/char.js`): composed from the existing seated pose: short thighs, the near leg hanging straight down, of the far leg only the top of the thigh (the rest is behind the plank), both arms forward. No new frames were drawn.
- **Art** (`src/art/props/park.js`): the seesaw takes `p.tilt` (-2..2; none = the old picture); the swing set takes `p.s0` / `p.s1` (each seat's offset; none = the old picture). Exported helpers `seesawZ`, `swingRise`, `SEESAW_SEATS`.

Not changed: the spring rider (still a "Sit" seat with the bench menu, static) and the slide (no seat, not usable). Neither looked wrong the way the seesaw did; a second rider on the seesaw's far end is supported by the code (city folk who sit there are moved with their end) but the crowd does not send anyone to sit there, so in practice the far end is empty.
