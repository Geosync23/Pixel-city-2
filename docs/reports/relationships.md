## What is built
- **`G.rel`** (`src/sim/rel.js` + helpers). State, the two tracks, remembered choices and details, the scene runner on the Chapters dialogue sheet, the pacing governor, invitations, the rose marker, the debug menu.
- **The scene runner.** A scene (85 vignettes, 110 romance scenes, 10 first dates) is compiled to a flat list with a saved program counter (`G.state.rel.cur`), exactly as the job beats are: it keeps its place on any line and at a choice across "Let's pick this up later" and save / load. Steps: `say / choice / if / beat / fx / play`. Conditions and effects are the kit's documented lists (`COND_PREFIXES`, `EFFECT_KEYS`). While a scene runs no need falls below 30. Everybody who speaks in a scene stands in it (Felix in Mr. Pike's shed): people who are already in the room are used as they are, somebody across the room or behind a counter comes over, somebody who is not there gets a figure beside the player (the Chapters' companion code, `_chapters_cast.js comp`).
- **Invitations.** The governor offers a scene: the person's `invite` text arrives in Messages, and on the first day the scene can be played a pinned Idea appears in My Day ("Mr. Pike, the old tram shed · They are expecting you"). Add makes it an appointment with them (tagged "With a friend" / "Family"). During the scene's window the person stands at the place; a **rose lantern with a heart** (not the Chapters' gold bookmark) marks the spot in the city; walking up to them, tapping them, or entering the room opens the Chapters' start card **in rose**: Begin / Not now. Nothing expires: Not now keeps it, a dismissed or missed Idea comes back after three days with no second notification. `place: 'home'` = they come to yours at the appointment time; `'phone'` = Dad rings (phone style); `'any'` = wherever the verb was used; `'theirs'` = their own home room, or (Marlow, Tasha, Noor, Tav) the flat, or their door.
- **The pacing governor** (`PACING` as data): one deep scene offered every 2-4 days; at most two invitations waiting; none on a day one was played, on a day a Chapter was begun, on a day an off-shift job beat was offered, or on a day a date is booked; nothing in the first five days; three friend / family arcs and two romances move at once (the others wait; highest friendship first); one person never invites twice in two days; arc gaps (`when.days`, default 3); soft conditions lapse at `orDays`; family scenes never run ahead of their Chapter. No scene starts during a Chapter, a family moment, a shift, a shift beat, a class, a show, a ride, Decorate or another window.
- **Small bits** (`_rel_small.js`): texts through Messages (at most 3 a day, 1 a person, 90 minutes apart, never 21:30-07:30; the day's budget is 2-4 bits, 2 on a work day with a Chapter open, 1 a day in the first five days and on a Chapter day; "Fewer messages" halves it); street greetings as the line in the social system's existing proximity bubble (never a notification, at most 4 a day, 1 a person); remembered details and loved-gift notes as the second line of the next talk, once each, a day later at the earliest. No-repeat bags per person and kind; the most specific bit that fits (stage, then context) wins. Family reactions (`FAMILY_REACTS`) and news answers (`NEWS`) arrive as texts a few hours apart.
- **The 25 context verbs** (`_rel_verbs.js`) in the existing talk panel, each only while its moment is true, with both meters on the talk sheet (friendship in pink with "Confidant" when earned; romance in amber with its stage, hidden until Something There) and on the person's card in Messages (plus the question you can ask next time, keepsakes from them, their page).
- **Dates as real play** (`_rel_dates.js`, `_rel_play.js`): all 16 date types. Booking (type > place > time inside their free hours) from the talk panel or Porchlight lands on My Day; the companion waits at the place, then stands and walks with you in their date look; arrive > their line > THE PLAY > their line > the talk (the first unplayed of their four conversations whose condition holds; then one comfortable line; "we do this now" from the fourth time) > close > goodnight text. Quiet scoring (fine / lovely / one for the books: warmth 4 / 6 / 8, a moodlet). Late: their kind line, -1. Not turning up: a kind text, -4 and a quiet "Make it up to them" Idea (a text of two honest lines, a loved gift or simply talking to them clears it; the Idea goes by itself after three days).
- **Porchlight** (`src/ui/phone_porchlight.js` + css, `src/art/rel/icons.js`): Cards (the stack of those who fit who you are open to), a card (portrait in their date look on a backdrop of their district, headline, About, three prompts with "Reply to this", three picture tiles, Looking for, Say hello, Not for me), Chats (they answer a hello a few game hours later, with `chat.known` or `chat.fresh`; up to four things to say; Suggest meeting after two exchanges: type > six-day strip > time chips > Ask), Me (your card, three prompts, open to, looking for, Pause my card, People I hid, Fewer messages). A hello that is answered opens the track at Something There and makes a stranger an Acquaintance. At "Together" it asks once whether to pause.
- **The romance track to the end** (`_rel_life.js`): Together (the others you were dating get a kind closing conversation in person, you choose the words), Partner, the key (a keepsake each), **moving in**, the proposal (the verb only appears when the answer is yes), **the wedding** at the six venues, married life, anniversaries; **kind endings** (three honest wordings, their own answer, the track closes, friendship -8 but never below Friend, ten quiet days, a 28-day wait before it can reopen, "tried before" remembered).
- **Living together**: the partner is at your home whenever their own cast routine has them at home or nowhere (the social system walks them in and out through the door); asleep in your bed at their own hours; one morning line a day; on their `cooksOn` days two plates of a dish of theirs are in your fridge when you come in; when you cook two servings in the evening they eat with you (+1 warmth, +3 for a favourite); a chore line every second day and the home gets cleaner (`G.supplies.addClean`); the single-bed line once plus a "A bigger bed" Idea; the tiny-home line once; Social drifts up of an evening; **40% of rent and utilities** paid in on bill day through `G.money.add` ("From Haru: their share": it is on the Bay Bank statement); "Stay in tonight".
- **The flat** (`src/scenes/rooms/rel_flat.js`, `src/art/rel/flat.js`): one 7 x 6 room template dressed four ways (rooms `rel_flat_marlow / _tasha / _noor / _tav`) with six small new props (a music stand, a cello on its stand, a cork tile with a race number, the crew photograph, a window on the cafe queue, a folded blanket). When one of the four heart scenes begins at the person's door you are let in, and seen back out to the door afterwards.
- **Pages and keepsakes**: the 22 order slips open as a little book from the person's card in Messages ("Their page"); the 26 keepsakes are listed there with their descriptions (gold notice when given).
- **The two optional favours**: "Feed the master stock" at Haru's counter (three days, between his second and third heart scenes) and "Feed Birdie's flock" by the duck pond (10:00-13:00, four days, after `birdie.rota`): one tap each, they set `haru_fed_broth` / `birdie_fed`.
- **Play ids that did not exist**: `minigame:fixit` turned out to exist (the handiness game of the skill kit, `G.skillplay` 'fixit'): it is used as it is. `activity:post_send` was built as a small real activity (stand in the queue, hand the parcel over: about eight game minutes). `activity:swim` is the swimming mini-game. Every play has the quiet "Not today".

## Known gaps
- "The old tram shed" is a street corner in the city (the cast spot is in the roadway; there is no shed, no tram). Mr. Pike now stands on the pavement beside it. Several other `spot:` places are bare corners too; the captions describe more than the eye sees.
- A date, the wedding and an ending keep no place across a reload (they start again); only the 205 written scenes do.
- Guests at the wedding stand in a ring round the player (the companion code), not in rows on chairs; no confetti; the lamp is not lit at Gull Point. City Hall's ceremony runs in the Clerk's room (the room whose name matches "wedding").
- The partner's things (`LIVEIN.brings`) are named in the gold notice, not placed as furniture; "visibly through the front door" is the social system's ordinary walk-out, not a staged one; the morning item on the counter is a line, not an object; a `fix` chore does not mend a fixture (all chores raise cleanliness).
- Keepsakes are list entries, pages have their own little book (lead patches 9, 12). The two recipe effects teach the recipe through `G.cooking.learn`.
- The standing weekly thing of a Confidant (`PEOPLE[id].standing`) is not offered in My Day. "Trade spare keys" sets the fact and says the line; drop-ins and plant-watering are not built.
- The `advice` verb looks only at: a date within two days, a promotion or hiring within two, a move within seven, a Chapter within two.
- The talk panel can show fifteen or more buttons for a romanceable Friend (it fits 390 x 844; it is a long list).
- One line was written by me because the data has none: Dez's one mention of Porchlight ("Cuz. There's an app the whole depot is on. Porchlight. I'm not on it. I'm on it. Do NOT look me up.", `rel.js`, the `day` handler). The start card's fallback hook ("They are expecting you. It will take a little while, and it keeps your place."), the "together" prompt words, the wedding end card and a few toasts are mine too.
- The social system still fades a friendship by a point after four days without a word (its own rule, not changed); scenes count as a talk.
- Pace numbers (warmth per date, thresholds, the 40%) are the designer's, untuned. In the Haru run warmth reached 85 a week after Partner: the calendar (14 and 28 days as partners), not warmth, was what paced the end of the track.
