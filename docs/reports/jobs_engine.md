## What is built
- **The beat runner** (`src/sim/jobstory.js`, `G.jobStory`). Reads `ARCS`, checks the documented triggers at clock-in, plays a beat's steps through the Chapters dialogue sheet (`ui/chapters_ui.js`: portrait, typed text, choices as buttons, the Log), applies the effects, sets `beat:<id>`. A beat is compiled to a flat list with a saved program counter, so it survives save / load on any line and at a choice.
- **Rules that hold:** at most one beat a shift; not on two shifts running (except beats 1 and 2); a `clockin` beat plays before the card, a `mid` beat at a lull about 42% into a worked shift, a `clockout` beat before the pay card; a beat that does not get its moment (skipped, auto-worked, left early) waits; soft conditions lapse at `orShifts`; no beat starts while a Chapter is being played; quitting freezes the arc and coming back resumes it (counters, picks and done beats are kept per job, for good).
- **The clock-in card** (owner direction): "Work the shift" / "Skip to the end", every shift, every job. Skip is the existing auto-work: full hours paid at the flat score, the shift counts as attended (for the job and for the story). After a beat has waited through two shifts a third button appears: "Just the moment, then skip". When a beat is due the card carries one quiet line ("There is something in the air today.").
- **11 new jobs** (the ones that need no new room): gate agent, baggage handler, station agent, retail associate, museum guide, florist, aquarist, cook at the Gilded Gull, hotel bell, observatory guide, hair stylist. Listings, hiring, shifts, the four-level ladders, boss texts (the boss is a named person who is not in cast.js: texts arrive under their name), a ticket script for the work loop each, stations on existing fixtures (no room file was edited, see "Stations"), a look for every speaker, shift clothes from existing outfit art. The four `needsRoom` jobs (radio DJ, teacher, reporter, firefighter) are not listed; their arcs already run in the engine.
- **Staff door + staff pass** (found in play-testing): the Aquarium and the Conservatory shut their public door before 9:00 and the Aquarium walks visitors out before 8:00, but the aquarist and the florist start at 7:00; the Museum, Aquarium, Observatory and Conservatory turn people without a day ticket back from the wings where the stations are. For the half hour before a shift and during it the door of your own workplace lets you in, the room does not walk its own staff out, and clocking in stamps the day ticket. All done from `jobstory.js` through those systems' public hooks and state (`door.onUse`, the door interactable's `use`, `scene._closing`, `G.state.<venue>.ticketDay`).
- **The plays**: 21, one for every live job (`src/sim/_jobplay_games.js` on the small kit `_jobplay_kit.js`). A play appears once per worked shift as one **pink ticket** ("★ The Regulars") at one of the job's stations, from 30% of the shift, never on a shift with a mid beat. Tapping it opens a bottom sheet (the game is paused: a play costs no shift time). Every sheet has **Auto** (the usual result, 0.5, at once) and **x** (close: the ticket stays). The result (stars at 0.55 / 0.85, the designer's good / bad line) counts as three tasks' worth of task score, pays a bonus of $1.50 to $4.50, adds to the tip quality and gives up to 18 xp in the job's main skill. With "mini-games off" the ticket resolves at 0.5 without a sheet. Nothing about a play can block pay.
- **Ambient shift lines** (`SHIFT_LINES`, 22-23 per job): a no-repeat shuffle bag per job, saved; conditional lines are only drawn while their condition holds. Up to three of the job's people who are not cast.js characters stand in the room during a worked shift (near the stations; tap one and they say a line of their own); cast people who are in the room speak for themselves; a line from somebody who is not in the room, or whose head is under the HUD, is shown as a caption with their name. One line every 12 to 20 real seconds, never two of ours at once (`TUNE.lineEvery`).
- **Off-shift beats** (7): an invitation. The invite text arrives (texts: at most one from work a day, never between 21:30 and 07:30), a pinned Idea appears in My Day on the day of the window ("Mr. Lindgren and Marlow, Saturday afternoon · They are expecting you"), Add makes it an appointment, and the scene plays when the player is at the place inside the window. It never expires; the next beat waits for it until that beat's `orShifts` (or six further shifts) and then goes on. A source is also registered with `G.myDay.addSource` for the case that the pin is missing.
- **Effects as data**: rel (cast people through `G.social.addRel(raw)`, everybody else in `G.state.jobs.story.rel`), flag (`G.state.flags`), xp, money, mood (the five `MOODLETS_NEW` are registered into `MOODLETS`), perf (added to the shift score, clamped -5..+10), text, opp (the 21 flag-gated `OPPS` rows join the normal Opportunity pool at load; the others are offered by the beat), item (keepsakes: a gold notice + `G.jobStory.keepsakes()`, listed in the Jobs app), need.
- **Jobs app** (`ui/phone_jobs.js`): the Board lists the entry jobs ("Hiring now") and then every other career ("More careers"); My Job shows the arc so far (title, shifts you showed up for, the beats seen by title, the play and its best result, keepsakes) without saying what is next.
- **Dev**: `G.jobStory.debug` (below) and a ready chooser `G.jobStory.debug.menu()`.

## API, events, state
`G.jobStory = { clockIn(job, shift, cb), shiftEnd(summary, show, quiet), addRel(id, d)` (called by `sim/jobs.js`) `, arc(jobId) -> { title, premise, shifts, plays, best, beats: [{ id, n, title, at, done, invited, due }] }, next(jobId?) -> beat | null, rel(id), pick(choiceId), flag(id), keepsakes() -> [{ id, job, name, kind, desc }], play(jobId?) -> Promise<score | null>, hasPlay(jobId), playName(jobId), active(), test(cond), TUNE, debug }`
`debug = { list(jobId?), fire(beatId) -> Promise, setShifts(jobId, n), reset(jobId?), play(jobId), clockIn(jobId, level?)` (hire, go there, clock in the way the player does) `, stations(jobId?)` (inside the room: how many fixtures each station resolved to) `, line(jobId?), texts()` (deliver waiting texts now) `, today(), next(jobId, n?), state(), menu() }`
Events: `jobstory:beat:start`(beatId), `jobstory:beat`(beatId), `jobstory:choice`(choiceId, optionId), `jobstory:invite`(beatId), `jobstory:play`(jobId, score), `jobstory:keepsake`(itemId).
State (inside the jobs slice, so old saves simply gain it): `G.state.jobs.story = { v, jobs: { [jobId]: { shifts, day, done: { beatId: shiftNo }, last, wait, plays, best, mem } }, picks, rel, keeps, q: [{ id, at }], sent: { textId: day }, textDay, cur: { id, job, pc } | null, bags: { jobId: [line indices] }, off: { beatId: { n, t, pin, done? } } }`. Flags live in `G.state.flags`.
`G.jobs` gained: `roomOf(job)`, `job(id)`, `clockInNow()`, `debugStart(jobId, level?, { story: true })`; a job row may carry `room` (the station room when it is not `venue`), `boss`, `hoursNote`, `wear` (shift clothes; `uniform` stays empty for new jobs so that `_crowd_rooms.js` does not re-dress a venue's ambient staff), `isNew`. `_jobs_work.js` gained `inject({ title, task, run, onDone })` (one extra ticket whose step opens `run`).

## How to add things
- **A beat**: add a `B(...)` to the job's arc in `src/story/jobs_?.js`; run `node tools/jobs_check.mjs`. Nothing else: the engine reads the data.
- **A job in an existing room**: its row in `jobs_new.js` (remove `needsRoom`), a row in `STATIONS` and a ticket script in `src/sim/_jobstory_stations.js` (selector = an existing function tag or an asset-id regex, optionally the nearest one to a point, or `'@'` for a floor spot), a shift outfit in `UNIFORM_ART` (`sim/jobs.js`). In the room, `G.jobStory.debug.stations(jobId)` shows what resolved.
- **A job in a new room** (the four that wait): build the room with the job's station tags on its fixtures, then as above with selectors that are simply those tags.
- **A play**: `PLAY[jobId] = async (ctx, env) => score` in `_jobplay_games.js` from the kit's parts (`ctx.pick`, `ctx.order`, `ctx.grid`, `ctx.live`, `ctx.mg(def)` for a `ui/minigame.js` game, `ctx.skill(key)` for a `G.skillplay` game), plus its station in `STATION`.
- **A speaker's real look**: a row in `LOOKS` in `_jobstory_cast.js` (keys of the character art spec). Without one the look is derived: a seeded citizen adjusted by the words of the designer's `look` brief.
