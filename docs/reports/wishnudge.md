# Wish-list reminder (owner msg 175)
Worker: Opus, 2026-11-16. Owner said yes to "a wish-list reminder when walking past the shop" (instore.md, owner question 2).

## What it does
When you **walk** in the city within 4.5 units of the door of an **open** shop that stocks an in-store-only piece on your **Roomy wish list** or your **Closet wish list**, one phone banner drops in:

- **On your wish list** / "Hanging Egg Chair, Rattan is sold here at Warp & Weft."
- Several wished pieces at that shop: one line, "Supernova Tie-Dye Tee +1 more: sold here at Thread & Co. Outlet."
- **Tap the banner:** the player walks to that shop's door and goes in (the same as tapping the door: `G.useInteractable` on the city door). If no door is found it falls back to `G.instore.take` (the Map's "How will you get there?" card).
- The entry in the phone's notification history opens Roomy on the piece (home goods) or the Closet wish list (clothes). The x on the banner dismisses it.

It reuses the phone's own banner (`G.phone.notify`), so it sits under the HUD like every other banner, respects the Settings "banners" switch and lands in the notification history.

## Rules
- Once per piece per game day.
- At most one reminder per shop visit. A visit ends when you are 6.5 units away. Walking out of that shop's own door counts as a visit already, so there is no reminder as you leave it.
- Closed shop: silent for that visit.
- Never while any of these is on:
  - a Chapter (or a Chapter moment), a relationship scene or date (`G.rel.active`);
  - a job shift or a job story beat;
  - the intro, a cutscene or an activity (`G.busy`);
  - a ride (`G.transit.ride`) or a cab on its way (`G.transit.pending`);
  - design mode, the phone, any window (`G.modal`) or a mini-game;
  - another phone banner. The reminder then waits and comes once that banner has gone, if you are still near.
- Never for a piece you own: clothes via `G.wardrobe.owns`; home goods when placed, in storage, in a box, pending, in the shopping bag or in a parcel on its way.
- Never for a piece that is no longer on the wish list, or that can be ordered online.
- Clothes count only when they are in season and on the rails there this week (or at their anchor shop).
- Not when the guide arrow already points at that shop.
- Banner length: the banner shows two lines. A line over 66 characters uses the store's short name ("Bellwether"), then drops the shop name ("... is sold here."). I measured every possible line (3,623 lines from the real catalogue, in the real banner at 390 px): none is cut off. 54 of them fall back to having no shop name.

## Cost (phones)
- The shop -> wished pieces index is rebuilt only when it changes:
  - The signature (both wish lists, the stock week and the season) is compared at most once per game minute.
  - The events `order:wish`, `goods:buy`, `wardrobe:add`, `shop:buy` and `load` also mark it for a rebuild.
- The walk check runs every 0.5 s of real time, in the city only, over the few indexed shops (typically 2-10). It does not scan the catalogue.

## API
`G.wishNudge` (`src/sim/wishnudge.js`, order 136):
- `index()` -> `[{ place, venue, shop, kind: 'good'|'piece', x, y, ids }]`
- `check()` -> the reminder shown or null
- `go(placeId)`
- `text(name, more, shop, short?)`
- `state()`
- `NEAR` 4.5, `LEAVE` 6.5, `FIT` 66
- `debug.reset()`

Event: `'wish:nudge'(placeId, [pieceId])`.

## State
`G.state.wishnudge = { d: day, s: [pieceId reminded today] }`.
- It is created on first use, so old saves load fine (tested with `tools/perf_save.json`).
- It is reset when the day changes, and pruned to wished pieces whenever the index is rebuilt.
- Visits are in memory only (not saved).
