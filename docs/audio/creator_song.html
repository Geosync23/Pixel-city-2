Creator Song

Three candidates for the tune that plays while you make your character and pick your first home (instead of the city sounds). All three are written in the old engine of the eight Classics, with the same instruments, and play here through the game's own code at the game's music level. Each one loops until you tap Stop; the first time round always has the tune and the drums, later times round now and then drop the tune for a rippling chord, as the Classics do. Pick the one you like; it is a one-word change in the game.

Bright and bouncy, the most "video game" of the three: a square-wave hook that climbs up and skips back down, rim clicks and an octave-hopping bass.

C major · 120 bpm · 32 s a time round (8-bar hook + 8-bar B part), then it loops

Skipping and a little jazzy: a soft triangle tune with a swing, seventh chords like Daytime Streets, a fuller snare and a walking bass.

F major · 112 bpm · 34 s a time round (8-bar hook + 8-bar B part), then it loops

The fastest and most excited: a short punchy hook with a jump up at the end of each bar, a steady light kick on every beat, a running bass.

A major · 126 bpm · 30 s a time round (8-bar hook + 8-bar B part), then it loops
