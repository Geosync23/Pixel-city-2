Plane Sounds

The three new airport sounds, played by the game's own sound code. Nothing here is a recording: all of it is made on the spot from shaped noise and tones. Tap a button to listen; headphones help for the far-away one.

Standing by the runway, near the touchdown point: the airliner comes in (about 5 s), touches down, reverse thrust, then rolls out and fades. About 11 s. Jet only.

The same landing at the game's volume for downtown (about 250 tiles away): a faint low rumble. Turn your volume up to hear it. About 11 s.

The main wheels touching the runway: two quick chirps and a soft thump. About 0.3 s.

From 2 s before touchdown to 3 s after, by the runway: the screech lands exactly as the wheels touch, under the jet. About 5 s.

The cabin "ding dong" when the scene cuts inside the plane: a high soft bell, then one a major third lower. About 0.9 s (the tail rings a little longer).

Other takes to compare
