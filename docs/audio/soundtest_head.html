City sound test, take 2

Real recordings in the background, changing over time, and a small clean sound for each thing you can see: a putter for cars, soft steps, bells. No white noise anywhere. Tap the scene to move the listener (the white ring); sounds come from the left or right and fade with distance. Headphones help.

Sound is off.

The background is five real recordings (public domain or CC0). It crossfades to a different recording every 40 to 70 seconds, starting from a random point, so it does not sound like one loop. The on-screen sounds are pure tones, with no hiss in any of them. In the game they would follow the real cars, people and places.
